## Scope

Home page (`/`) plus the shared public shell (header, footer, search) that the catalog, category and profile pages inherit. Visitor mode: Persuade on `/`; the catalog, profile, dashboards and admin are Operate surfaces inside the same world.

## Audience and job

Bulgarian household clients in София/Пловдив/Варна/Бургас/Русе who need a job done on a particular day; secondary: craftsmen deciding whether to list themselves. Action: search (what + city + date) and open a craftsman; secondary: "Стани майстор" registration.

## Proof and content

Real product mechanism only: live craftsmen rows from the database with their 14-day availability rule and headline price. No invented statistics, testimonials or user counts. Demo data is synthetic.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Time is measured like a job. Every craftsman carries a folding-rule strip of the next 14 days; free days are inked, busy days left bare. Refuses the category default of a photo-card carousel with a big blue search hero.

OWN-WORLD: Enamel rule-yellow (#F2C200) owns whole bands (search band, rule strips, the craftsman CTA field); ink black (#16140F) for type and graduations; red (#D2261E) only for decimeter numerals and the "today" tick; free-day green (#2F6B4F) as the availability ink; neutral concrete-paper ground (#F3F2ED; cream is the category default and deliberately refused), warm card surface (#FFFDF8). Components: segmented rule strips with hinge dots every 7 days, square-ish 6px corners, hairline graduations, Sofia Sans + Sofia Sans Extra Condensed for numerals at poster scale.

STORY: A visitor understands in one viewport that this catalog answers "who can, how much, when free", believes it because real rows show real availability, and searches or opens a craftsman.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left/top: monumental extra-condensed headline "Кой е свободен в сряда?" (weekday auto-swaps to a near date), subline with the promise. Beneath: the search band laid on a full-width yellow rule (what / city / date), primary button "Търси". Right/below on mobile: three live craftsman rows, each with its 14-day rule. Graduation ticks run along the band's lower edge.

FORM: Folding carpenter's rule (сгъваем дърводелски метър), candidate 4 of 7, seed key 56d68e0b. Raises: one day-graduation unit everywhere (oscilloscope), monumental numerals (alphabet storm), charged empty yellow on free space (ikebana).

Signature interaction: the rule unfolds. On the profile and calendar the 14-day strip opens segment by segment on first view (hinge rotation, 7-day sections), and choosing a date in search slides a red marker along the rule. Motion grammar: short hinge rotations with exponential ease-out; reduced motion shows the strip flat.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
